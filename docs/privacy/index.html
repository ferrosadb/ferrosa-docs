Ferrosa AI for iPhone

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes what the Ferrosa AI app for iPhone (the “app”) collects, how information is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how to request access or deletion. The app is made by Ferrosa, Inc. (“Ferrosa,” “we,” “us”). This policy also covers test builds distributed through Apple’s TestFlight. The initial App Store release is offered in the United States.

If you use the app through your employer or another organization, that organization may operate the services you connect to and have its own privacy policy. A written agreement with us may also govern information we process on its behalf, subject to applicable law.

1. How the app works

The app connects to machines on which you or your organization run Ferrosa Memory. It joins them as an authenticated peer over an encrypted connection. Your notes, memory contents, and session history can be stored on your own machines and, where applicable, on your phone. If you choose a paid Ferrosa service that stores your data, that data is stored on shared infrastructure operated by Ferrosa. We receive and store the content you choose to save to those services and use it to provide the service you selected.

Our account and connection services help your phone and your machines find and authenticate each other. Establishing a connection involves exchanging network addresses and connection information. Direct connections are the default. If your account includes an optional relay and a direct connection is unavailable, encrypted connection traffic can pass through a relay we operate.

If your organization operates its own console or relay, it operates those services and controls the information they handle. Services and AI providers you or your organization connect to may receive content you send to them.

2. Information we collect

Account and device pairing

When you pair your phone through the Ferrosa console, account and pairing information includes your account email address, a public key generated on your phone, and a device label. The device label may be the name you have given your phone. Account services also process information supplied by the sign-in provider you use and the information needed to authenticate and manage your account and paired devices.

The corresponding device private key is stored on your phone. The app does not receive the password you use to sign in to an external identity provider.

Diagnostics

Builds configured for error reporting send diagnostics to Sentry to help us find and fix problems. Reports can include a randomly generated installation identifier, app version, error details, and stack traces. The installation identifier is separate from your account and device hardware identifiers.

The app configures Sentry to exclude account identity, screenshots, view hierarchies, and automatic network breadcrumbs, and scrubs diagnostic context before sending it. Like other online services, a diagnostic service must process network information to receive a request.

TestFlight and support

Apple may share tester information, crash information, and feedback with us when you use TestFlight. Information can include the name and email address associated with an invitation, device and operating system details, and comments or screenshots you choose to send.

When you contact support, we receive your email address, your message, attachments you send, and information needed to respond. Please avoid sending passwords, keys, or sensitive content.

3. Information on your phone and your machines

Voice and typed notes

Voice-note transcription runs on your phone. The app downloads a speech-recognition model when needed; downloading a model requires a connection to the model’s hosting service. The transcript is saved to the Ferrosa Memory system you connect to, which may be a system you operate or a paid Ferrosa-hosted service you choose. Typed notes and associated context, such as the screen you were on, app version, and device type, are also sent to that system.

Passwords, keys, and local data

The app uses the iPhone Keychain for sensitive credentials such as device keys and saved connection credentials. Where Face ID is used, authentication is handled by iOS; the app does not receive your facial biometric data. The app can also keep an encrypted local copy of knowledge data, protected by the app’s available authentication controls.

Services you connect

If you connect an external service or AI provider, information you send through that service is handled according to your configuration and that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Your organization’s administrator may control these settings and access to your machines.

4. How we use information

We use account and pairing information to authenticate you, manage your devices, and connect the app to your machines. We use diagnostics, beta feedback, and support messages to investigate issues, improve reliability, and respond to you. We also use necessary service records to protect accounts, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who receives information

Providers that help deliver the app and related services process information needed for their functions. These include Apple for app distribution and TestFlight, Sentry for configured diagnostics, the identity provider you choose for sign-in, and providers that host our account, connection, and optional paid storage services.

We may disclose information when required by law or where necessary to protect the security of the service or the rights and safety of people. Services you or your organization operate or choose to connect are governed by your arrangements with their operators.

6. Retention and your choices

If you choose to save data to a paid Ferrosa-hosted service, we store it on shared infrastructure while your account is active and delete that hosted data when you close your account. We retain account and pairing information while it is needed to provide your account and manage device access. We retain diagnostic, connection, and support records only for as long as needed for their stated purposes, including investigating problems, protecting the service, and complying with legal obligations. Some security or legal records may need to be retained after an account is closed.

You can request a copy or deletion of personal information we hold by emailing support@ferrosa.ai. We may verify your identity before responding. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to correct information, restrict or object to its use, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator.

Signing out removes account details from the phone. The app’s sign-out and erase action removes local account access and revokes gateway device access. Device revocation disables the affected devices’ access; it does not close your account or delete hosted content. Gateway revocation records may remain for security and audit purposes. To close your account and delete data stored in paid Ferrosa services, contact us using the address above. Information stored on your own machines is managed separately. Ask the operator of those machines about deleting notes, memory, and session history or changing retention settings.

You can change app permissions, including microphone access, in your iPhone’s Settings. Information already submitted to an external provider is subject to that provider’s retention and deletion practices. If your organization has an agreement with us, its retention and deletion terms also apply to information we process on its behalf, subject to applicable law.

7. Children

The app is intended for professional use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or ask for consent.
