How can we help?

Get help with the Ferrosa AI app, connecting to your machines, and managing your data.

Email support@ferrosa.ai

Contact support

For app support, account questions, or a bug report, email support@ferrosa.ai. If you use Ferrosa through an organization, your administrator can also help with machine access and permissions.

To help us investigate, include:

  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version or TestFlight build number.
  • What you were trying to do, what happened, and any error message.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and roughly when it happened, including your time zone.

Please remove private content from screenshots or logs before sending them. Never send passwords, private keys, access tokens, pairing codes, or sensitive notes.

Trouble connecting to a machine?

  1. Check that your iPhone has internet access and the machine is powered on and connected.
  2. Confirm that Ferrosa Memory is running on the machine and that you have paired the app with the intended account and machine.
  3. If access is managed by your organization, ask your administrator to check your device and access permissions.
  4. Reopen the app and try connecting again. If it still fails, send support the error shown in the app.

A connection problem does not mean that the data on your machine has been deleted. Avoid erasing or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step.

Voice notes and microphone access

Voice notes require microphone permission and a downloaded transcription model. Allow the model download to finish before recording. If microphone access was denied, you can change it in your iPhone’s Settings under Privacy & Security > Microphone.

If a download fails, check your connection and available storage, then retry in the app.

Account, device, and privacy requests

For a copy of information Ferrosa holds about you, or to request its deletion, email support@ferrosa.ai from the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Signing out, erasing the app’s local account access, or revoking gateway devices does not close your Ferrosa account. To close your account and delete data you chose to save in paid Ferrosa-hosted services, contact support using the address above. Data in those services is stored on shared Ferrosa infrastructure and is deleted on account closure. Notes, memory, and session history on machines you or your organization operate are managed on those machines; contact your administrator if you need help removing that data.

See our privacy policy for details about the information the app handles.

Testing with TestFlight?

You can send beta feedback through TestFlight or email us directly. Include the build number and redact any private information in screenshots.
